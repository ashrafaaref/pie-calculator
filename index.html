How to read these estimates

Lower / central / upper are hypothetical scenarios, not probability bounds or guaranteed worst / best outcomes. Actual losses can exceed the lower case. Smooth growth omits market crashes and sequence risk. Rates are nominal before tax, fees, purification and currency effects.

Defaults are −2%, 6% and 10% annual total return, 2% illustrative dividend yield and 2.5% inflation. These are adjustable planning inputs, not estimates derived from the pie holdings. Dividend oriented and growth oriented each offer A (strict) and B (tolerant). These are portfolio and screening choices, not risk ratings. All four initially share the same illustrative rates and yield; changing the selector alone does not change the projection. Enter suitable assumptions for each plan before comparing. Currency selection changes the display unit; it does not convert money or model exchange rates.

Returns compound monthly; contributions arrive at month end. Reinvest for N whole years, then take all subsequent dividends out. Dividend withdrawals use beginning-of-month balance × annual yield / 12, capped at the available balance. Reinvested dividends are already inside total return and are never added twice. Constant yield is a simplification; distributions may be cut.

Four pie choices: Dividend oriented / Strict A, Dividend oriented / Tolerant B, Growth oriented / Strict A, Growth oriented / Tolerant B. Separate verified allocation-to-return mappings are not yet available. This calculator models aggregate capital and does not place orders or certify holdings.

Method references: Investor.gov compound interest calculator · Vanguard: total return.
